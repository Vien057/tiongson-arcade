The Experience

More than a place to eat.

Tiongson Arcade is presented as a waterfront gathering place where the day's catch arrives fresh at dawn and every meal comes with a view. Our promise is simple: fresh, honestly sourced food, transparent pricing, and genuine hospitality.

Our Story

Fresh catch. Local stories. A shared table.

The food featured in the tour is not presented as food created only for visitors. Grilled tuna, kinilaw, and sinigang connect the dining experience to fishermen, families, recipes shared across generations, and GenSan's relationship with the sea.

As the sun sets over the waterfront, the setting becomes part of the experience — a place to gather, celebrate, and simply be together.
